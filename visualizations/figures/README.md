# Manuscript figures

Open a figure title to see the artwork and complete panel caption together. The plotted-values CSV identifies the panel and data series. For replicate points, the `observation` column identifies the physical microplate, flask, tube, batch, or reference sample. Points at identical coordinates can overlap. Each caption identifies the unit used for its uncertainty estimate. Absorbance at 380 nm and the thymol blue pH ratio serve as indicators of the metabolic byproducts Fe³⁺ and H⁺ in cultures. In abiotic standards, the same readouts characterize the response to ferric iron and pH.
