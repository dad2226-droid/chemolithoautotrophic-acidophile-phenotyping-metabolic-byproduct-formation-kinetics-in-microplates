# Table 3. Compatibility tests of absorbance indicators in chemolithoautotrophic acidophile media.

Added Fe₂(SO₄)₃ concentrations exclude the Fe already present in the medium. Measured pH values describe medium–standard mixtures before dye addition. Medium-component concentrations refer to the standard medium before dye addition. All measurements were abiotic six-wavelength absorbance measurements in non-UV-transparent microplates without lids.

| Experiment ID | Experimental family and purpose | Experimental factors (number of values or conditions) | Replication and measurement details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [AFM-01](#sources-for-afm-01) | Compatibility: Fe³⁺ indicator | AFM1 and AFM3 with 0–5.2×10⁻² M added Fe₂(SO₄)₃, without dye (8 [Fe₂(SO₄)₃] × 2 media) | AFM1 and AFM3 occupy separate well groups on the same plates |
| [F2S-01](#sources-for-f2s-01) | Compatibility: ratiometric pH and Fe³⁺ indicators | F2S with 0–5.2×10⁻² M added Fe₂(SO₄)₃, without dye (8 [Fe₂(SO₄)₃])<br><br>F2S supplemented with H₂SO₄, spanning measured pH 0.7–2.1, with and without 10⁻⁵ M thymol blue (8 [H₂SO₄] × 2 [dye]) | Fe₂(SO₄)₃ and H₂SO₄ standard series occupy separate well groups on the same plates |
| [Starkey-01](#sources-for-starkey-01) | Compatibility: ratiometric pH indicator | Starkey (St) medium with brown or yellow S⁰, supplemented with H₂SO₄, spanning measured pH 0.7–2.8 for each medium, with and without 10⁻⁵ M thymol blue (8 [H₂SO₄] × 2 S⁰ types × 2 [dye]) | Both S⁰ types occupy separate well groups on the same plates |
| [9K-01](#sources-for-9k-01) | Compatibility: ratiometric pH indicator | AFM4 or AFM5 supplemented with H₂SO₄, spanning measured pH 0.7–3.8 for each medium, with and without 10⁻⁵ M thymol blue (8 [H₂SO₄] × 2 media × 2 [dye]) | AFM4 and AFM5 occupy separate well groups on the same plates |
