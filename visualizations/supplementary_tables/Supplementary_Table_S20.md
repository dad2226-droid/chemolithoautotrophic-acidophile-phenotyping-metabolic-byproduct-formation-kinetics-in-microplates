# Supplementary Table S20. Soluble-sulfur endpoint ratio contrasts

Measured biotic-minus-abiotic ratio changes are small or variable across the tested thiosulfate and tetrathionate cultures. Counts distinguish all candidate plates from plates with eligible scheduled endpoints.

**Figure panels** 8a–f · [All Supplementary tables](README.md)

**Column definitions**

- `cohort` — Baseline no-addition group defined by sulfur substrate and organism (AF for Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans or AT for Acidithiobacillus thiooxidans), with nominal substrate concentration or initial OD600 as indicated.
- `readout` — Identifies the measured thymol-blue ratio R = A545/A485 or its scatter-corrected counterpart R&#42; = (A545 − A750)/(A485 − A750), where the numbers denote absorbance wavelengths in nm.
- `candidate_plate_n` — Number of physical microplates scheduled for endpoint assessment in this cohort and readout before requiring both biotic and abiotic values.
- `paired_endpoint_plate_n` — Number of microplates with finite biotic and matched abiotic changes for this readout at the final scheduled acquisition.
- `batch_n` — Number of distinct batches represented by the microplates with paired biotic and abiotic endpoint changes.
- `inoculum_culture_n` — Number of distinct inoculum cultures represented by the microplates with paired endpoint changes.
- `net_endpoint_change` — Mean across eligible microplates of the biotic endpoint-minus-initial ratio change minus the corresponding abiotic change, in dimensionless ratio units.
- `net_between_microplate_sd` — Sample standard deviation of net endpoint ratio changes across eligible microplates.
- `minimum_net_change` — Smallest net endpoint ratio change observed among eligible microplates, in ratio units.
- `maximum_net_change` — Largest net endpoint ratio change observed among eligible microplates, in ratio units.
- `endpoint_min_d` — Earliest final scheduled acquisition among the eligible microplates, in days from the initial acquisition.
- `endpoint_max_d` — Latest final scheduled acquisition among the eligible microplates, in days from the initial acquisition.
- `marginal_eligible_endpoint_plate_n` — Number of microplates eligible for this readout’s biotic–abiotic endpoint contrast regardless of eligibility for the other readout.
- `common_eligible_endpoint_plate_n` — Number of microplates eligible for both measured and corrected biotic–abiotic endpoint contrasts at the same scheduled acquisition.
- `status` — Identifies a descriptive comparison among independent microplates from a single acquisition-date group.
