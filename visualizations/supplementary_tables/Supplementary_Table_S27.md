# Supplementary Table S27. Response span, precision and separation across handling conditions

A similar span across temperature, plate and membrane conditions can coexist with a different pooled within-standard SD or signed adjacent-standard separation. Folds summarize span and SD. Local separation is a signed score difference.

**Figure panels** 2e–h, 3g–l · [All Supplementary tables](README.md)

**Column definitions**

- `assay` — Identifies whether the handling comparison used the pH indicator or the ferric iron absorbance indicator.
- `readout` — Measured or scatter-corrected pH ratio (R or R&#42;) or 380 nm absorbance (A380 or A&#42;380) used for the comparison.
- `dye` — Nominal thymol blue concentration in the standards (µM).
- `material` — Microplate material for the candidate acquisition, identified as UV-transparent or non-UV-transparent.
- `contrast` — Pair of handling conditions being compared. The candidate appears first and its reference second, including the two acquisitions used for membrane and lid comparisons.
- `step_id` — Identifier of the candidate incubation or acquisition step. The lid-present/lid-absent comparison has no separate incubation-step identifier.
- `acid_M` — Nominal sulfuric acid concentration (M) where a single acid background defines the calibration series.
- `paired_plate_n` — Total number of paired candidate and reference microplate comparisons in this row, including pairs lacking an estimate for a particular metric.
- `span_plate_n` — Number of plate pairs with positive finite response spans for both conditions.
- `span_fold_estimate` — Geometric mean of candidate/reference response-span ratios across eligible plate pairs. Each span is the maximum minus minimum mean response among common complete standard levels.
- `span_fold_ci_low` — Lower limit of the 95% confidence interval for the span fold, obtained from a Student’s t interval on the plate-pair log₂ ratios and transformed back.
- `span_fold_ci_high` — Upper limit of the 95% confidence interval for the span fold, obtained from a Student’s t interval on the plate-pair log₂ ratios and transformed back.
- `sd_plate_n` — Number of plate pairs with positive finite pooled within-standard SDs for both conditions.
- `sd_fold_estimate` — Geometric mean of candidate/reference technical SD ratios across eligible plate pairs. Each technical SD pools well variances across common complete standard levels, weighted by their degrees of freedom.
- `sd_fold_ci_low` — Lower limit of the 95% confidence interval for the technical SD fold, obtained from a Student’s t interval on the plate-pair log₂ ratios and transformed back.
- `sd_fold_ci_high` — Upper limit of the 95% confidence interval for the technical SD fold, obtained from a Student’s t interval on the plate-pair log₂ ratios and transformed back.
- `local_plate_n` — Number of plate pairs with an estimable local separation score in both handling conditions.
- `local_estimate` — Mean paired difference in local separation (candidate minus reference). For each plate, divide each eligible adjacent change by √[(SD_low² + SD_high²)/2], assign positive scores to decreasing pH signal or increasing iron signal as the reference increases, then take the median score.
- `local_ci_low` — Lower limit of the pointwise 95% Student’s t confidence interval across plate pairs for the mean difference in local separation.
- `local_ci_high` — Upper limit of the pointwise 95% Student’s t confidence interval across plate pairs for the mean difference in local separation.
- `reference_reversal_plate_n` — Number of plate pairs in which the reference condition had at least one eligible adjacent interval with a response change opposite to the expected direction.
- `candidate_reversal_plate_n` — Number of plate pairs in which the candidate condition had at least one eligible adjacent interval with a response change opposite to the expected direction.
- `span_status` — Whether the span fold was estimated from multiple plate pairs, available for one pair without a confidence interval, or unavailable.
- `sd_status` — Whether the technical SD fold was estimated from multiple plate pairs, available for one pair without a confidence interval, or unavailable.
- `local_status` — Whether the local separation difference was estimated from multiple plate pairs, available for one pair without a confidence interval, or unavailable.

Blank numeric cells indicate quantities that were unavailable or inapplicable for that row.
