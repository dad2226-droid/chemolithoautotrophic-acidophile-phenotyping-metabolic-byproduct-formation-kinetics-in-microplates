# Supplementary Table S17. Plate–flask correspondence in AUC and timing

Correspondence varies by treatment for both within-format control-normalized AUC and time to first observed maximum. The folds compare the plate quantity with its flask counterpart. The match status distinguishes exact and neighboring doses.

**Figure panels** 7e–f · [All Supplementary tables](README.md)

**Column definitions**

- `comparison_quantity` — Identifies mean AUC relative to each format’s no-additive control or mean elapsed time to the first observed maximum. Plate A380 and flask apparent Fe³⁺ generation are the underlying signals.
- `additive` — Identifies the substance used for dose matching. Al, Cu, Ga and VO denote sulfate salts of those cations (VO is vanadyl). NaCl and citric acid identify those compounds.
- `plate_dose_mM` — Reported concentration of the matched additive in the microplate, in mM on a cation basis for metal sulfates.
- `flask_dose_mM` — Reported concentration of the matched additive in the shake flask, on the same mM basis as the plate dose.
- `Ga_mM` — Additional Ga³⁺ concentration (mM) in a citric acid formulation. When Ga is the matched additive, its exposure is given by the plate and flask dose columns.
- `match_status` — Whether the reported doses were equal or the microplate dose was the nearest available dose below or above the flask dose.
- `comparison_context_n` — Number of attempted pairings of a microplate formulation and starting batch with a flask cohort at the listed doses. A cohort can occur in several pairings.
- `defined_context_n` — Number of attempted pairings with a finite log₂ ratio for the specified quantity, requiring positive, available quantities in both formats.
- `plate_batch_n` — Number of distinct microplate starting batches represented in pairings with a defined ratio.
- `flask_batch_n` — Number of distinct shake-flask starting batches represented in pairings with a defined ratio.
- `plate_vessel_n` — Number of distinct physical microplates recorded in pairings with a defined ratio, counting each plate identifier once.
- `flask_vessel_n` — Number of distinct physical shake flasks recorded in pairings with a defined ratio, counting each flask identifier once.
- `median_log2_fold` — Median across defined pairings of log₂(plate quantity/flask quantity). Zero denotes equality and +1 denotes a twofold larger plate quantity.
- `q25_log2_fold` — 25th percentile of the defined log₂ plate/flask ratios across pairings.
- `q75_log2_fold` — 75th percentile of the defined log₂ plate/flask ratios across pairings.
- `median_fold` — Median log₂ ratio converted to the fold scale as 2 raised to that median. A value of 1 denotes equality between formats.
- `minimum_log2_fold` — Smallest defined log₂ plate/flask ratio among the pairings.
- `maximum_log2_fold` — Largest defined log₂ plate/flask ratio among the pairings.
- `final_read_maximum_context_n` — Number of defined pairings in which at least one treated plate or flask trajectory reached its first observed maximum at the final acquisition.
- `status` — Indicates a descriptive comparison across shared cohorts and controls, or an unavailable ratio because a quantity was missing or nonpositive.

Blank numeric cells indicate quantities that were unavailable or inapplicable for that row.
