# Supplementary Table S23. Within- and between-batch agreement of iron screens

Event fractions are generally consistent across batches. Counts distinguish agreement, discordance and insufficient information at each comparison level.

**Figure panels** 5a · [All Supplementary tables](README.md)

**Column definitions**

- `species` — Inoculated organism, identified as AF for Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans or LF for Leptospirillum ferriphilum.
- `stratum` — Group of conditions defined by the added-compound class or absence of additions.
- `comparison_level` — Within a starting batch, compares binary outcomes across plates at the same condition. Between batches, compares the fractions of plates that passed at that condition. A plate passes when a postbaseline biotic A380 increase of at least 1 AU coincides with a matched abiotic increase below 1 AU.
- `candidate_comparison_n` — Number of repeated groups at the indicated level, counting condition–batch groups with at least two physical plates or conditions represented by at least two batches.
- `evaluable_comparison_n` — Number of candidate groups for which every contributing plate could be classified as event-positive or a complete no-event observation.
- `incomplete_comparison_n` — Number of candidate groups containing at least one unevaluable plate decision.
- `identical_event_fraction_n` — Number of evaluable groups with identical outcomes across plates within batch or identical positive-plate fractions across batches.
- `identical_event_fraction_percent` — Percentage of evaluable groups with a zero range in plate outcomes or batch positive-plate fractions.
- `event_fraction_range_median_pp` — Median group range among evaluable comparisons, in percentage points. Within a batch the range is 0 or 100 points across plate outcomes. Between batches it is the highest minus the lowest percentage of plates passing.
- `event_fraction_range_q25_pp` — 25th percentile of the group ranges in event frequency across evaluable comparisons, in percentage points.
- `event_fraction_range_q75_pp` — 75th percentile of the group ranges in event frequency across evaluable comparisons, in percentage points.
- `event_fraction_range_max_pp` — Largest group range in event frequency among evaluable comparisons, in percentage points.
- `all_positive_comparison_n` — Number of evaluable groups in which every constituent plate screened positive.
- `all_negative_comparison_n` — Number of evaluable groups in which every constituent plate had a complete no-event result.
- `identical_mixed_comparison_n` — Number of between-batch comparisons in which every batch had the same positive-plate fraction strictly between zero and one.
- `discordant_comparison_n` — Number of evaluable groups in which plate outcomes within a batch or positive-plate fractions between batches differed.
- `disagreement_percent` — Percentage of evaluable groups with a nonzero range in plate outcomes or batch positive-plate fractions.
- `status` — Indicates whether repeated groups supported a descriptive consistency estimate or no repeated group was evaluable.

Blank numeric cells indicate quantities that were unavailable or inapplicable for that row.
