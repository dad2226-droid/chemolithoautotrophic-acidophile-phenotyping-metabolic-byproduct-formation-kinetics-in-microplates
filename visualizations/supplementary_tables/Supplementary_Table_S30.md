# Supplementary Table S30. AUC comparisons over a common observation window

Using the common observation interval changes plate and flask control-relative AUC estimates. Changes are percentage points relative to the corresponding time-to-maximum calculation.

**Figure panels** 7e–f · [All Supplementary tables](README.md)

**Column definitions**

- `additive` — Treatment component whose concentration varies. Metal symbols denote the cation supplied by its salt.
- `plate_dose_mM` — Reported microplate concentration of the variable additive in mM, expressed on a cation basis for metal salts.
- `flask_dose_mM` — Nominal shake-flask concentration of the variable additive in mM, on the same chemical basis as the plate dose.
- `Ga_mM` — Additional Ga(III) concentration in mM for the citric acid series. For the gallium dose series itself, the doses appear in the plate and flask dose columns.
- `match_status` — Whether the reported doses are equal or the microplate dose is the nearest available dose below or above the flask dose.
- `candidate_context_n` — Number of distinct plate-batch and flask-condition comparisons identified for this formulation and dose pair before window eligibility is checked.
- `paired_context_n` — Number of comparisons in which both formats had an eligible primary response and all primary-eligible vessels also had a calculable common-window response.
- `unavailable_context_n` — Number of candidate comparisons lacking complete paired primary and common-window estimates in both formats.
- `plate_batch_n` — Number of distinct microplate batches in all candidate comparisons for the dose pair.
- `flask_batch_n` — Number of distinct shake-flask batches in all candidate comparisons for the dose pair.
- `plate_response_change_pp` — Mean change in microplate control-relative AUC, in percentage points, using the shared window instead of integrating each trajectory to its own first maximum. Response is 100 times treated AUC divided by mean control AUC. Vessel results are averaged within comparisons before averaging across comparisons.
- `flask_response_change_pp` — Corresponding mean change in flask control-relative AUC, in percentage points, calculated as shared-window response minus response to each trajectory’s first maximum.
- `format_difference_change_pp` — Change in the plate-minus-flask response difference, in percentage points, calculated as the shared-window difference minus the primary difference.
- `primary_format_difference_pp` — Mean microplate response minus flask response, in percentage points, when each treatment and control trajectory is integrated to its own first observed maximum.
- `fixed_format_difference_pp` — Mean microplate response minus flask response, in percentage points, when all treatment and control trajectories in a comparison are integrated over their shared observation window.
- `window_min_d` — Earliest available common ending day among candidate comparisons. Each comparison ends at the earliest final reading of its plate, flask, and assigned control trajectories.
- `window_max_d` — Latest available common ending day among candidate comparisons, in days from incubation start.
- `status` — Identifies a descriptive window-sensitivity comparison with shared controls and cohorts. No population-level test was performed.

Blank numeric cells indicate quantities that were unavailable or inapplicable for that row.
