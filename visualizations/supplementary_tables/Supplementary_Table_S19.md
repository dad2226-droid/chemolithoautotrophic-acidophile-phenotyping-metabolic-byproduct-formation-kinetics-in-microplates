# Supplementary Table S19. Fresh-starter culture-tube endpoint contrasts

Fresh L. ferriphilum starters give positive ORP contrasts under some conditions and higher apparent OD600. A. ferrooxidans and A. thiooxidans cultures supplied with 5 mM tetrathionate have lower final pH and higher apparent OD600 than their abiotic controls.

**Figure panels** 7h–i, 8g–h · [All Supplementary tables](README.md)

**Column definitions**

- `cohort` — Identifies either the fresh-starter L. ferriphilum experiment in HH medium at 37 °C or the tetrathionate tube experiment at 30 °C.
- `organism` — Inoculated organism compared with matched abiotic tubes. LF denotes Leptospirillum ferriphilum, AF denotes Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans, and AT denotes Acidithiobacillus thiooxidans.
- `Ga_mM` — Nominal Ga concentration in the biotic and matched abiotic tube treatment, in mM on a Ga cation basis.
- `In_mM` — Nominal In concentration in the biotic and matched abiotic tube treatment, in mM on an In cation basis.
- `metric` — Identifies the final measurement as oxidation–reduction potential versus Ag/AgCl (3 M KCl), measured pH, or fluorescence-derived apparent optical density at 600 nm using the AF calibration.
- `unit` — Unit for the endpoint means, standard deviations, and difference, given as mV for oxidation–reduction potential, pH units for pH, or absorbance units (AU) for apparent OD600.
- `endpoint_d` — Mean elapsed time in days of the inoculated tubes at their recorded final sampling event.
- `independent_batch_n` — Number of distinct experimental batches represented by the inoculated tubes in this comparison.
- `biotic_starter_n` — Number of distinct inoculum cultures used for the inoculated tubes in this comparison.
- `biotic_n` — Number of distinct inoculated tubes with an eligible final measurement for the indicated metric.
- `abiotic_n` — Number of distinct uninoculated tubes with an eligible final measurement in the matched batch, medium, treatment, temperature, and sampling event.
- `biotic_mean` — Arithmetic mean of the eligible inoculated-tube endpoint measurements in the stated unit.
- `biotic_sd` — Sample standard deviation among the eligible inoculated-tube endpoint measurements in the stated unit.
- `abiotic_mean` — Arithmetic mean of the eligible matched abiotic-tube endpoint measurements in the stated unit.
- `abiotic_sd` — Sample standard deviation among the eligible matched abiotic-tube endpoint measurements in the stated unit.
- `biotic_minus_abiotic` — Mean inoculated-tube endpoint minus the matched abiotic-tube mean in the stated unit. A positive value indicates a higher final measurement in the inoculated tubes.
- `status` — Identifies descriptive endpoint comparisons from one starting cohort with a shared starting-medium reference.
