# Supplementary Table S28. Eligibility of measured and scatter-corrected ratios

Corrected ratios fail eligibility more often in some optical configurations and A. ferrooxidans sulfur treatments. Counts include each acquired well and read. Percentages are averaged across physical plates with equal weight.

**Figure panels** 2b–c, 4a–b, 8a–f · [All Supplementary tables](README.md)

**Column definitions**

- `cohort` — Identifies whether the ratio eligibility counts describe optical standards or sulfur microplate cultures.
- `experiment_id` — Experimental series identifier linking the acquired well measurements to the deposit’s experimental records.
- `plate_type` — UV-transparent or non-UV-transparent plate designation for optical standards. This field is blank for the sulfur culture rows.
- `medium_id` — Medium identifier when an optical standard series is subdivided by growth medium.
- `plate_n` — Number of distinct physical microplates contributing acquired well measurements to this row.
- `attempt_n` — Total acquired well measurements across those plates, counting each well once per acquisition even when neither ratio was eligible.
- `raw_valid_n` — Number of acquired well measurements yielding an eligible measured ratio, R = A545/A485.
- `corrected_valid_n` — Number of acquired well measurements yielding an eligible corrected ratio, R&#42; = (A545 − A750)/(A485 − A750).
- `both_valid_n` — Number of acquired well measurements for which both the measured and scatter-corrected ratios were eligible.
- `raw_only_n` — Number of acquired well measurements for which only the measured ratio was eligible.
- `corrected_only_n` — Number of acquired well measurements for which only the scatter-corrected ratio was eligible.
- `neither_valid_n` — Number of acquired well measurements for which neither ratio was eligible.
- `raw_percent_mean` — Mean across plates of the percentage of each plate’s acquired well measurements yielding an eligible measured ratio, with equal weight per plate.
- `corrected_percent_mean` — Mean across plates of the percentage of each plate’s acquired well measurements yielding an eligible corrected ratio, with equal weight per plate.
- `difference_pp_mean` — Mean of the plate-specific differences in eligible percentages (corrected minus measured), in percentage points.
- `difference_pp_min` — Smallest plate-specific difference in eligible percentages (corrected minus measured), in percentage points.
- `difference_pp_max` — Largest plate-specific difference in eligible percentages (corrected minus measured), in percentage points.
- `culture_type` — Biotic or abiotic well designation for the sulfur microplate cultures. This field is blank for optical standards.

Blank numeric cells indicate quantities that were unavailable or inapplicable for that row.
