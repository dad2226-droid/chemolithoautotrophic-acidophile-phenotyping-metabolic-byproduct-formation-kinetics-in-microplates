# Supplementary Table S9. Indicator drift during benchtop storage

Mean final ferric-indicator changes are small relative to initial spans, while some individual concentrations show larger drift. Deviations also occur among standards measured throughout storage. The interval type distinguishes confidence limits from observed ranges.

**Figure panels** 2b–c, 3b–e · [All Supplementary tables](README.md)

**Column definitions**

- `assay` — Identifies the thymol-blue absorbance ratio assay for pH or the ferric absorbance assay for iron.
- `dye` — Nominal concentration of added thymol blue, in micromoles per liter (µM).
- `readout` — The optical signal used in the calculation. R = A545/A485 and R&#42; = (A545 − A750)/(A485 − A750). A380 is absorbance at 380 nm and A&#42;380 = A380 − A750.
- `metric` — Drift summary for each plate. Endpoint rows use final minus initial signal as a percentage of the initial span, or log₂(final/initial) for positive signals. Trajectory rows use the mean across post-initial acquisitions, the largest absolute acquisition mean, the largest absolute single-standard drift, or the root-mean-square drift across standard–acquisition pairs. Percentage drift uses the plate’s initial response span.
- `endpoint_day` — Mean elapsed time in days from the initial acquisition to the final recorded acquisition across physical plates.
- `plate_n` — Number of physical plates summarized for this assay, dye, and readout. In endpoint and common-support analyses, only plates with a finite value for the stated metric contribute to this count.
- `estimate` — Arithmetic mean of the plate-level drift summaries, in log₂-fold units for log₂(final/initial) and percent of the initial response span for all other metrics.
- `sd` — Sample standard deviation of the plate-level endpoint drift, in the units of the metric. Reported for the available-final-values rows.
- `interval_low` — Lower 95% confidence limit for mean endpoint drift, or the smallest observed plate summary for descriptive trajectory metrics, as identified by interval_type.
- `interval_high` — Upper 95% confidence limit for mean endpoint drift, or the largest observed plate summary for descriptive trajectory metrics, as identified by interval_type.
- `p_value` — Unadjusted two-sided one-sample t-test p-value for zero mean endpoint drift, reported only for the available-final-values rows.
- `p_holm` — P-value after Holm adjustment across the 12 available-final-values tests of signed change or log₂ fold change.
- `multiplicity_family_n` — Number of endpoint tests with finite p-values in the Holm adjustment family. Blank for rows without a test.
- `status` — Whether the support analysis estimated an endpoint mean with an interval or reported a descriptive summary. Blank where the source trajectory summary supplied no status.
- `support_basis` — Standards and acquisitions entering the calculation. Available-final-values rows use the final acquisition. Available-level-and-acquisition rows use eligible post-initial measurements. In the sensitivity comparison, available_case uses each readout’s available levels at each acquisition and fixed_common_levels uses only levels valid for both readouts at every acquired index, including the initial read.
- `interval_type` — Identifies a two-sided 95% Student’s t confidence interval across physical plates or the observed minimum-to-maximum range of plate summaries.
- `fixed_common_level_n` — Total plate–standard combinations that met the fixed common-level rule, summed across plates. In the sensitivity analysis this count is reported for both available-case and fixed-support rows.

Blank numeric cells indicate quantities that were unavailable or inapplicable for that row.
