# Supplementary Table S22. Apparent iron-oxidation screen outcomes

Most no-additive A. ferrooxidans controls satisfy the simultaneous screening criteria. Some positive screens have net biotic changes below 1 AU. The evaluable denominator includes positive events and complete negative observations.

**Figure panels** 5a · [All Supplementary tables](README.md)

**Column definitions**

- `species` — Inoculated organism, identified as AF for Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans or LF for Leptospirillum ferriphilum.
- `stratum` — Grouping of plate conditions by added-compound class or absence of additions. The no-addition group includes the recorded preparation and pH settings.
- `candidate_plate_condition_n` — Number of distinct physical microplate–condition combinations considered for screening, counting each plate and treatment column once across recorded acquisitions.
- `physical_plate_n` — Number of distinct physical microplates contributing candidate conditions to this group.
- `starter_batch_n` — Number of distinct starting batches represented by the candidate plate conditions.
- `evaluable_decision_n` — Number of plate conditions with either an observed event or a complete paired record establishing no event.
- `unevaluable_decision_n` — Number of candidate plate conditions with no observed event and too little paired biotic and abiotic coverage to classify them as negative.
- `event_n` — Number of plate conditions with at least one postbaseline acquisition at which mean biotic A380 rose by at least 1 AU and matched abiotic A380 rose by less than 1 AU from their respective initial readings.
- `event_percent` — Percentage of evaluable plate conditions that met the event criterion at least once.
- `no_event_n` — Number of plate conditions with no event, a finite initial pair, and finite biotic and abiotic values at every recorded postbaseline acquisition, including at least one such acquisition.
- `partial_no_event_n` — Number of unevaluable plate conditions with no observed event and at least one finite postbaseline biotic–abiotic pair, but incomplete coverage of the initial or postbaseline pairs.
- `first_event_net_min_AU` — Smallest net change in A380 at the first qualifying acquisition across event-positive plate conditions, in AU. Net change is the biotic change from baseline minus the matched abiotic change.
- `first_event_net_q25_AU` — 25th percentile of net A380 changes at the first qualifying acquisition for each event-positive plate condition, in AU.
- `first_event_net_median_AU` — Median net A380 change at the first qualifying acquisition for each event-positive plate condition, in AU.
- `first_event_net_q75_AU` — 75th percentile of net A380 changes at the first qualifying acquisition for each event-positive plate condition, in AU.
- `event_without_net_1AU_n` — Number of event-positive plate conditions whose net biotic-minus-abiotic A380 change remained below 1 AU at every qualifying acquisition.
- `event_without_net_1AU_percent` — Percentage of event-positive plate conditions whose net A380 change remained below 1 AU at every qualifying acquisition.
- `observed_window_median_d` — Median elapsed time to the last recorded postbaseline acquisition across candidate plate conditions with a recorded duration, in days.
- `status` — Identifies descriptive classifications of the recorded optical screen for apparent iron oxidation.

Blank numeric cells indicate quantities that were unavailable or inapplicable for that row.
