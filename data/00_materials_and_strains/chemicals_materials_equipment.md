Additive-stock solubility and stability checks were performed and verified as specified in [biological_definitions.yaml](../03_biological_microplates/biological_definitions.yaml) under `stock_preparation.solubility_screen`. Nominal stocks were checked for visible solids and a ≤0.05 absolute pH change over 7 d. The protocol used a 0.2 µm filter.
