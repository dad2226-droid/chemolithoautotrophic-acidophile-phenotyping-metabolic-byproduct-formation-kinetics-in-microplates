[strains.yaml](strains.yaml) contains strain identities and their experimental collection assignments. ATCC/DSM designations identify strains. `Ab` is an abiotic-control code. Indicator calibration and medium validation contain no organisms.

Only independent physical microplates are biological replicates. Within each microplate, E, F and G were inoculated from 3 separately grown cultures; other replicate microplates used independent sets of 3 cultures. Flask starter assignments and tube `inoculum_culture_ID` records identify their biological replicates. The LF microplate starters and their follow-up are separate from the freshly thawed test-tube starters. Incubation temperature and medium are experimental-condition fields.
